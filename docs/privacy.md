# Privacy Policy for Cloud Editor

**Last Updated:** 01/25/2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Cloud Editor ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our application within the Google Workspace Marketplace.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Google User Data
Our application integrates with Google Workspace services (such as Google Drive™). When you use Cloud Editor, we may request access to specific Google user data, including:
* **File Access:** We access the files you explicitly open with Cloud Editor to allow you to view and edit them.
* **Profile Information:** We may access your basic profile information (email address, name) to authenticate your session.

### B. Usage Data
We may collect anonymous usage data (e.g., error logs, performance metrics) to improve the stability of our application.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect strictly to provide the service functionality:
* To read and write files you have authorized us to access.
* To maintain your session and preferences.
* To troubleshoot technical issues.

**We do not use your data for advertising purposes.**

## 4. Data Retention and Sharing
* **Retention:** We do not store your file content on our servers permanently. Files are processed in memory or temporary storage only for the duration of your editing session.
* **Sharing:** We do not share, sell, or transfer your personal data to third parties, except as required by law.

## 5. Google API Services User Data Policy
Cloud Editor's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 6. Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data during transmission and processing. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
